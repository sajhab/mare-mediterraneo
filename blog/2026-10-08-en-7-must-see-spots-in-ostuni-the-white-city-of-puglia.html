7 Must-See Spots in Ostuni — The White City of Puglia

Explore Ostuni, the White City of Puglia, with our insider guide to its stunning architecture, delicious cuisine, and more. Perfect for your Salento adventure!

Photo by Tania Lyahnovich on Unsplash

1. Explore the Historic Old Town

As you step into Ostuni, the dazzling White City of Puglia, be prepared to wander through its maze of narrow, winding streets. The historic old town, or ‘Centro Storico’, is a postcard-perfect scene of whitewashed buildings, charming arches, and staircases that seem to lead you on a delightful mystery tour. Make sure to visit the 15th-century Ostuni Cathedral, a stunning example of Romanesque-Gothic architecture.

2. Visit the Stunning Piazza della Libertà

In the heart of Ostuni, Piazza della Libertà is where locals and visitors alike gather. This vibrant square is home to the impressive Town Hall (Palazzo San Francesco) and the Church of Saint Francis of Assisi. Stop by one of the nearby cafes for an espresso or gelato and soak in the lively atmosphere.

3. Experience Ostuni's Culinary Delights

Ostuni offers a feast for the senses with its array of traditional Apulian cuisine. Savor fresh seafood, homemade pasta, and local wines at delightful trattorias. Don't miss the chance to try the famous ‘burrata’ cheese, a delicacy that originated in the region. Whether dining in an upscale restaurant or a quaint eatery, the flavors here are unforgettable.

4. Take in the Views from the Belvedere

For breathtaking panoramic views of the Adriatic Sea and olive groves stretching as far as the eye can see, head to the Belvedere on the outskirts of the old town. This vantage point is perfect for a spectacular sunset or simply to admire the city's unique landscape.

5. Discover the Local Markets

If you're visiting on a Saturday, don't miss Ostuni's bustling market. From local produce to handmade crafts, it's a vibrant showcase of what Puglia has to offer. Engage in friendly banter with the vendors and bring home a piece of Ostuni with you, whether it's a bottle of olive oil or a beautifully crafted souvenir.

6. Relax on the Beaches Nearby

Just a short drive from Ostuni are some of Puglia’s most beautiful beaches. Relax on the sandy shores of Torre Pozzella or the crystal-clear waters of Lido Morelli.
