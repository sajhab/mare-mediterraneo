Discover Otranto: Italy's Easternmost Gem in the Heart of Salento

Otranto, the easternmost city in Italy, offers visitors a blend of history, beauty, and culture. Explore its stunning cathedral and enjoy the vibrant atmosphere—a perfect day trip from Mare Mediterraneo in Torre Chianca.

Photo by Paolo Chiabrando on Unsplash

Introduction to Otranto: A Gem of Salento

Nestled along the shimmering coastline of the Adriatic Sea, Otranto is an enchanting town in Salento, known for its pristine beaches, historic sites, and colorful town life. As the easternmost city in Italy, Otranto stands proudly with its ancient roots and stories echoing through the cobbled streets. With Mare Mediterraneo in Torre Chianca as your base, only 35 minutes away, you can easily immerse yourself in this captivating blend of history and sea breeze.

Discover the Remarkable Otranto Cathedral

Your Otranto travel guide must start with the Otranto Cathedral, a magnificent blend of art, history, and spirituality. Built in the 11th century, it boasts an intricate mosaic floor depicting the Tree of Life, a masterpiece of early medieval art. Walking through the cathedral is like turning the pages of a history book filled with the vibrant stories of times gone by.

Exploring Otranto: What Not to Miss

After exploring the cathedral, wander the labyrinth of narrow streets filled with quaint shops and inviting cafes. The Aragonese Castle, with its imposing towers and panoramic sea views, is another must-see. For those eager to absorb the local vibe, visiting during the lively summer festivals will provide a true taste of Salento culture.

A Day Trip to Remember: Otranto and Surroundings

Otranto makes for an unforgettable day trip, thanks to its proximity to Mare Mediterraneo. Just 35 minutes from your peaceful retreat in Torre Chianca, you’ll find an array of experiences. The clear waters of Baia dei Turchi, just outside the city, are perfect for a refreshing swim. Alternatively, immerse yourself in the tranquility at Punta Palascìa Lighthouse, Italy's easternmost point—a spot to experience surreal sunrises.
