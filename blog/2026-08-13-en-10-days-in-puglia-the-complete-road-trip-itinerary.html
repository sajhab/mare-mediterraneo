10 Days in Puglia: The Complete Road Trip Itinerary

Uncover the magic of Puglia with this 10-day road trip itinerary. Explore Lecce, Gallipoli, and beyond with insider tips from a local travel enthusiast.

Photo by Krzysztof on Unsplash

Day 1: Arrival in Torre Chianca

Welcome to Puglia, the enchanting heel of Italy's boot! Start your adventure in Torre Chianca, a charming coastal town just 400 meters from the stunning Adriatic Sea. This tranquil escape is only 15 minutes from Lecce, making it the perfect base for exploring the region. Consider staying at Mare Mediterraneo for a cozy retreat; their Suite Pietra and Suite Cielo are delightful starts to your journey.

Day 2: Exploring Lecce

Begin your second day in Lecce, a baroque masterpiece fondly known as the 'Florence of the South'. Wander through its narrow cobblestone streets and savor the grandeur of the Basilica di Santa Croce. Grab a traditional coffee at Alvino Café, and don’t miss the Roman Amphitheater. Lecce is where you'll feel the heartbeat of Puglia come alive.

Day 3: Beach Day at Punta Prosciutto

A mere 25-minute drive from Torre Chianca will lead you to the unspoiled paradise of Punta Prosciutto. Sink your toes into its white sandy beaches and take a refreshing swim in the crystal-clear waters. It's a perfect day for relaxation under the southern Italian sun.

Day 4: Discovering Porto Cesareo

Venture 30 minutes from Torre Chianca to Porto Cesareo, a lively harbor town famous for its seafood and vibrant marina life. Enjoy a leisurely lunch of fresh mussels or octopus at one of the waterfront trattorias, then explore the marine reserve by kayak or boat tour.

Day 5: Culture and Wine in Otranto

Head 35 minutes southeast to Otranto, a town steeped in history and charm. Visit the Otranto Cathedral to see the magnificent floor mosaic dating back to the 12th century. Afterward, sample local wines at one of the vineyards in the surrounding countryside.

Day 6: Discovering Gallipoli

A 45-minute drive will take you to Gallipoli, famed for its old town perched on a limestone island. Dive into its rich past at the Castello Angioino and indulge in fresh seafood at Il Bastione. Keep an eye on recent safety measures, as the night markets become lively and bustling with visitors and locals alike.

Day 7: Exploring Ugento

Make a day of exploring the serene beauty of Ugento, which is just a short drive inland. Visit the Archaeological Museum to see fascinating relics of the town's ancient past. Enjoy a quiet afternoon by the beach with crystal waters and picturesque views.

Day 8: Authentic Charm of Novoli

Travel to Novoli, where cultural experiences await. The town is vibrant with traditional festivals, and you might catch a glimpse of a historical reenactment if your timing is right. Taste their famous wines and participate in a vineyard tour, creating memories you'll cherish.

Day 9: Return to Lecce

On your penultimate day, return to Lecce to soak in any sights you might have missed. Indulge in some local shopping or take a leisurely walk through its lovely piazzas. Lecce's charm will surely invite you for a second visit.

Day 10: Departure from Torre Chianca

Your Puglia adventure concludes back in Torre Chianca, where you can reflect on this incredible journey by the sea, perhaps while enjoying an evening aperitivo on the beach.
