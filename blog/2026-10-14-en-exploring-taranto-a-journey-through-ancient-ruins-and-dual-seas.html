Exploring Taranto: A Journey through Ancient Ruins and Dual Seas

Experience Taranto, a city where two seas meet, offering a rich tapestry of history and stunning landscapes in Puglia. Dive into our travel guide and uncover its ancient Greek heritage.

Introduction to Taranto: A City Between Two Seas

Taranto, known as the city of two seas, is a charming and historical gem nestled on the Ionian coast of Puglia. Just over an hour's drive from Mare Mediterraneo in Torre Chianca, Taranto offers visitors a unique blend of stunning coastal views and historical depth. Its strategic location between the Mar Grande and Mar Piccolo provides a picturesque maritime backdrop, perfect for those seeking both natural beauty and cultural enrichment. Staying at Mare Mediterraneo, situated only 15 minutes from Lecce and 400 meters from the sea, positions you ideally for daily excursions to this fascinating city while enjoying the comforts of a home away from home.

Option 1: Exploring the Rich History of Taranto

Taranto is steeped in history, with its foundations in ancient Greek civilization. As part of Magna Graecia, the city boasts numerous archaeological sites that bring history to vibrant life. One must-visit site is the National Archaeological Museum of Taranto (MArTA). This museum is home to one of Italy's best collections of artifacts from the Magna Graecia period, offering a glimpse into the ancient world‘s past grandeur and artistic achievements.

  • Pros: Ideal for history enthusiasts and culture seekers, the museum provides a deep dive into ancient Greek influences in Puglia.
  • Cons: While rich in historical content, the museum is not geared toward young children, making it less suitable for families with young kids.

For those interested in the open-air historical experience, the ruins of the Greek Temple of Poseidon are a compelling stop. Although only two columns remain, these ruins are among the oldest Greek remains on mainland Italy, a profound testament to the longevity and influence of Grecian architecture.

Option 2: Embracing the Scenic Beauty of Taranto’s Seas

If you are more inclined towards natural beauty and leisurely seaside activities, Taranto’s unique geographical position offers abundant opportunities. You can enjoy a relaxing stroll along the Lungomare Vittorio Emanuele III, a coastal promenade with expansive views over the Mar Grande. Visiting the Aragonese Castle, accessible by a scenic walk, also provides both historical insights and breathtaking oceanic vistas.

  • Pros: Ideal for romantics and photographers, the scenic spots along the coast are perfect for capturing the essence of southern Italian beauty.
  • Cons: Some areas might become crowded during peak tourist seasons.

Recommendation: Tailoring Your Taranto Experience

For history buffs, the archaeological wonders and museum visits will make Taranto an unforgettable exploration of antiquity. If your heart lies with the sea, the stunning views and peaceful lapses along the coastline will offer the relaxation and inspiration that only Taranto can deliver. Each option provides its own unique experiences based on your interests.
